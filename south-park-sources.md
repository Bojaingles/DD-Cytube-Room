# South Park room assets and episode information

Episode data: https://www.tvmaze.com/shows/112/south-park and https://api.tvmaze.com/shows/112/episodes?specials=1 (retrieved October 15, 2026). TVmaze data is CC BY-SA; the episode panel links to TVmaze and its license. The local catalog retains source episode IDs, source links and summaries. Three display titles use the familiar broadcast titles Go God Go, Black Friday, and DikinBaus Hot Dogs. Explicit room-title aliases cover verified abbreviations/spelling variants; deleted scenes, the unedited pilot and music video do not receive an invented synopsis.

Header: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:South_Park_logo.svg (Comedy Central / South Park Studios; PD-textlogo, trademark remains). Color adjusted from black to light cream for the dark navigation. Transparent SVG hosted with the room code.

Tab icon: original small SVG depiction of an orange hood and eyes, embedded in the room profile. Existing DBN network artwork, support destinations and carousel artwork are preserved.
